How STUDX works

  1. Verify

    Sign up with your school email and student ID. Admins review each request.

  2. List or search

    Post an item or service, or describe what you need to SmartMatch.

  3. Connect on campus

    Message the student, agree on a meetup spot, and complete the deal.

  4. Rate

    Leave a review after completed deals to build trust for everyone.

STUDX SmartMatch

Tell us what you need and SmartMatch finds the best available student products or services.

How it works today: a transparent, rule-based ranking. It scores each listing on relevance, price, distance, rating, verification and availability. It is not a large AI model. The design allows an AI API to be added later.

Circular Campus

Every reused book, resold uniform or swapped gadget is one less thing thrown away. STUDX tracks books reused, uniforms resold, calculators reused, gadgets resold and items swapped.

These numbers are calculated from real completed transactions. We don't show made-up impact figures.

Trust & safety

  • Student verification reviewed by admins (Pending, Verified, Rejected)
  • No phone numbers or private contact details shown by default
  • Report scams, fake listings, harassment or suspicious accounts
  • Ratings only after completed transactions
